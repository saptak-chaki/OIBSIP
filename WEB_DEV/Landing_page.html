It started as an adda on a rooftop.

Cafe Roasters began with three friends, one second-hand roaster and a rooftop in North Kolkata. Friends kept asking for bags to take home, so we started packing them.

Today we roast in small batches twice a week. We still believe good coffee is best shared slowly, with long conversations and no hurry.
